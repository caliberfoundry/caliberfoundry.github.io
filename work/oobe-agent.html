What happens when the cost of a rebalance rises?

Financial technology instructors can use this five-period exercise to teach the relationship between trading costs, cash reserves and drawdown limits. All market conditions and decisions in the exercise are simulated.

  1. Open the simulator and compare the five periods at the default starting capital.
  2. Open the model inputs, select one period and change its fee or drawdown. The calculation carries the resulting balance into later periods.
  3. Download the JSON and compare the inputs, reserve, modeled costs and decision for each period. Reset the inputs to reproduce the original run.

The model uses five predefined venues with fixed scenario changes. It calculates simple yield between periods and applies a uniform shock to the simulated balance, including cash. It runs locally without a wallet or market feed.
