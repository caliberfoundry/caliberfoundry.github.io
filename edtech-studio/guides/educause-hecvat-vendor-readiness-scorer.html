Three Practices That Cut University Security Reviews From Months to Weeks

  • Proactive HECVAT + VPAT Bundle: Attaching a completed HECVAT workbook and ITI VPAT 2.5Rev ACR before the university asks eliminates 2–3 weeks of intake back-and-forth.
  • Cite Exact Policy Sections, Not Bare "Yes": Answering "Yes" without naming the SOC 2 criterion, penetration test date, or encryption cipher triggers follow-up clarification rounds.
  • Document Compensating Controls Honestly: Where a control is planned (e.g., InCommon federation vs. direct SAML 2.0), state the active compensating control instead of overclaiming.

Technical Drill-Down 01 — Selecting Between HECVAT Lite and HECVAT Full
  • HECVAT Lite (~95 questions): Designed for lower-risk cloud services processing non-sensitive or public/directory data without deep campus network integration.
  • HECVAT Full (~300+ questions across Organization, Policies, Architecture, IAM, Crypto, Change Mgmt, DR/BCP, FERPA/HIPAA/GDPR): Standard requirement when handling student education records, grades, financial aid, health records, or enterprise SSO/SIS integrations.
