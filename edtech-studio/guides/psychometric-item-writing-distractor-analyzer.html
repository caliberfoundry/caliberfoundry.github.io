Three Empirical Rules for High-Discrimination EdTech Items

  • Cover-the-Options Test: A domain-competent learner should be able to formulate the correct answer from the stem and scenario before reading Options A–D.
  • 3 Functional Options Beat 5 Padded Options: Meta-analyses by Rodriguez & Haladyna confirm 3–4 plausible options outperform 5-option items padded with joke distractors (<5% endorsement).
  • Eliminate Convergence & Length Cues: Keep all options parallel in grammar, length, and specificity, and never use All of the above (recognizing two true statements reveals the key).

Technical Drill-Down 01 — Kelley (1939) 27% Criterion Group Formulas

Rank examinees by total test score and isolate the top 27% (U correct out of N) and bottom 27% (L correct out of N):

Difficulty Index:      p = (U + L) / (2 * N)      // Target: 0.30 to 0.85 for norm/mastery discrimination
Discrimination Index:  D = (U - L) / N            // Target: D >= +0.30 (0.20–0.29 marginal; < 0.00 defective)
Active Distractor Rule: Selection rate >= 5.0% of total examinees and negative distractor point-biserial
