Privacy Policy

Caliber Foundry (also doing business as CaliberFoundry, "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal and corporate data in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and US FERPA guidelines for educational institutions.

1. Client-Side Zero-Retention Architecture

Our interactive browser workbenches (including CodeDrill™ CBT, DocuShield™ PDF/UA Studio, DealShield™, and ContentForge™) are engineered to inspect PDFs, security questionnaires, and exam progress locally inside your browser (via localStorage and in-memory JavaScript buffers). Uploaded PDFs and custom CSV questionnaires are not transmitted to or stored on third-party model training servers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Billing & Licensing Information: When you purchase a license or request a corporate invoice, we collect your name, business email address, company/organization name, and purchase order reference to deliver your license key and tax invoice.
  • Payment Card Data: Payment details are processed directly by our PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processors and Merchants of Record (Paddle.com, Gumroad, Inc., or Contra). Caliber Foundry never stores or accesses your full credit card number.
  • Anonymous Usage Telemetry: We collect minimal, non-invasive page view and feature interaction events to ensure application reliability and prevent abuse.

3. How We Use Information

We use your contact and billing information solely to provision software license keys, issue corporate tax invoices, deliver contracted engineering milestones, and respond to support requests sent to engineering@caliberfoundry.com. We never sell or rent customer data to third parties.

4. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal or corporate contact data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email engineering@caliberfoundry.com and we will complete your request within 72 hours.
