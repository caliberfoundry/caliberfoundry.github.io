# OmniRAG

[OmniRAG](https://caliberfoundry.com/omnirag/) reads searchable PDFs, UTF-8 text and delimited tables in the browser. Upload a pack, search for a term, open a cited passage and download the evidence. The PDF reader extracts a text layer and groups words by their position. Scanned pages need OCR beforehand. Check a returned table row against its original column headings. Numeric comparisons identify candidate differences for human review.

The free pack accepts two files. An individual Gumroad receipt unlocks up to twelve and an unredacted printable dossier. Files are processed by the browser. License activation contacts the configured purchase verifier. The shipped workbench uses local passage matching. Custom OCR and connector projects listed in the app are separately scoped services.

For development, serve the site root over HTTP and open /omnirag/.
